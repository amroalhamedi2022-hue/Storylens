# The Woman in White — actual 1860–1903 original art archive

**Fetched and committed:** ten independently downloaded/decoded images, all from original individually licensed Commons pages, during [GitHub Action 42384172037](https://github.com/amroalhamedi2022-hue/Storylens/actions/runs/42384172037). SHA256 checks refer to the **actual local WebP binary** in `plates/`. Metadata-only remote Wikimedia SHA1 is different; it does not stand in for a downloaded local source file.

## Edition-specific historical origin

**Work:** Wilkie Collins, *The Woman in White* (first serialized November 1859–1860). The British *All the Year Round* serialization and the original UK three-volume book **were not illustrated**. Source history: [Victorian Serial Novels research](https://victorianserialnovels.org/illustrations-woman-white/).

- **Eight Thomas Eyre Macklin lithographs (1903):** authentic work-specific 1903 prints, **not** presented as 1859/1860 British first-edition illustrations or as eight verified chapter scenes.
- **One 1861 frontispiece by Sir John Gilbert:** linked to the one-volume London Sampson Low edition, not the original three-volume edition.
- **One 1860 original Harper & Brothers title-bearing US cover:** the visible edition says **Illustrated by John McLenan**, but **John McLenan's 74 original American serialized illustrations are NOT stored in this ten-file archive**. This cover is archived as cover evidence and intentionally excluded from the report's chapter-art binary total.
- The existing complete English novel file `docs/literature/staging/user-requested-classics-20261105/the-woman-in-white/original-edition.html` was **already staged** from Gutenberg #583. We did not alter it, or claim it matches a specific 1903 print page-by-page.

All ten scans are staged **unapproved**. Individual Commons Public Domain metadata is recorded, but territory-specific legal clearance, manuscript/edition attribution and accurate chapter scene matching require human review before publication. The image processing preserves native source colouring, orientation and borders; no invented images, grayscale recolouring, artificial period styling or cropped substitutions.

## Binding distribution gates

- These ten originals are **archival sources**, not ten chapter-card candidates and not ready assets inside the Android APK.
- Neither the original cover nor the frontispiece is automatically assigned to a chapter; all eight Macklin images still need independent book-scene confirmation.
- No original novel paragraphs, translations, reader UI, signed package, app icons, finance logic or existing PR content were modified.
- Repeatable offline checks: `python tools/test_woman_white_original_art.py` and `python tools/report_chapter_art_progress.py`. Audit central report classifies **nine art files** and **one historical cover**, without mixing their counts.
- Before release, obtain a complete contextual Arabic translation and chapter segmentation, identify one distinct **scene-specific** historical plate per genuine chapter, confirm original print captions, provide accurate bilingual alt text and obtain human editorial + territorial rights approval.
