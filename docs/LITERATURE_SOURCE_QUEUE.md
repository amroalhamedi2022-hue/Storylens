## 2026-11-01 — Verified historical Arabic/English edition pairing (NOT packaged)

- **Kalila wa Dimna, Arabic 1816**: a genuine 18-chapter edition of Ibn al-Muqaffa's Arabic recension edited by Silvestre de Sacy, published Paris 1816: https://ar.wikisource.org/wiki/كليلة_ودمنة_(1816) . This work has earlier Indic/Pahlavi roots; "Arabic-origin" in our product means retaining the authentic chosen **Arabic literary recension**, not claiming the folktales originated in Arabic.
- **English 1819**: Wyndham Knatchbull historical translation of de Sacy's 1816 Arabic text, according to its preface and Smithsonian catalogue https://www.si.edu/object/siris_sil_342175 . Both edition indexes contain 18 numbered chapters in matching topical order: https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Kalila_and_Dimna_(1819) . **IMPORTANT**: the English Wikisource transcription contains missing/redlinked chapters (5, 6, 8 and 18 verified absent). The COMPLETE **1819 scan** is available as a book at https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Kalila_and_Dimna_(Hathi_hvd.32044009834078).pdf . Nothing has been imported or fully translated yet; alignment, completeness, any English modernization, legal review and in-page engraving rights remain OPEN. Do not claim 18 translated chapter bodies merely because an index lists 18!
- **Arabian Nights primary print**: Qatar National Library catalog records the 1835 Arabic Bulaq two-volume edition (found volume **2**) at https://ediscovery.qnl.qa/ar/islandora/object/QNL%3A00004954 . Must identify/obtain/compare both Arabic volumes with matching legally reusable full English edition (avoid children's selections). No content ingested yet.
