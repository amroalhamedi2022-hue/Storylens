## Requirements & implementation checklist — all 13
### RDR-01 — Genuine finger-held, deformable paper curl (CODEX)
Current `reader-experience.js` has `installPageTurn()`, `pageFoldGeometry()`, CSS clip/shadows and fallback gestures; user reports this does not function like holding a real physical page. Do **not** label it finished. Candidate **StPageFlip/page-flip v2.0.7 (MIT)** supports HTML pages, touch, portrait/landscape and soft/hard sheet; review API/limitations and licence, bundle locally, **not** from CDN at runtime. Ref: https://github.com/Nodlik/StPageFlip ; https://nodlik.github.io/StPageFlip/ ; https://www.npmjs.com/package/page-flip . Do not import turn.js without separate commercial licence audit (https://github.com/blasten/turn.js).
Acceptance: grab either page corner/edge, deform continuously under finger with genuine curved edge/belly/crease, multi-segment shading and **visible back side**, show real next/previous sheet behind, release > threshold completes with spring settle, release below threshold rebounds, stationary tap turns if designated, RTL/LTR correct, edge held across folded centre, no sideways jerk or blank frame. Both actual single and double spreads; handle cancelled/multiple touches, vertical scroll, word/illustration taps, slow/rapid successive turns, chapter boundaries, accessibility motion setting. Preserve real HTML text, original-colour art, dictionary tap and saved paragraph anchor. Never render the whole novel/huge DOM at once. **No cosmetic CSS-only transform as substitute for functional curl.**

### RDR-02 — Auto-rotation and real-book immersive landscape
Respect device auto-rotation/user preference; handle `configChanges=orientation|screenSize|keyboardHidden` in `AndroidManifest.xml` and WebView viewport resize without restarting chapter or losing text location. Landscape reading default = real centered **two-facing-page spread**, filling safe usable display area, proper left/right binding for source reading direction. Portrait = one physical page. In active landscape reading show ONLY both sheets, text and the deforming page; no always-visible header, progress or toolbar. Reveal brief minimal overlay via purposeful gesture if user needs exit/settings; hardware Back and accessibility navigation must always work. Screen rotation must preserve book/chapter/current paragraph, selected reader preset, saved words and translation state. No forced sensor override when device rotation locked.

### RDR-03 — Independent paper/material reading presets
App theme and **reader material** are separate preferences: (1) natural bound book (subtle gutter, depth), (2) authentic textured paper (light fibre/edge), (3) pristine white paper, (4) warm yellow/cream paper, (5) soft warm-grey eye-comfort, (6) low-glare night/sepia. Provide uncluttered chooser available from discreet overlay; persist independently; paper must retain clear opaque prose and genuine full-colour illustrations; no glass blur over ink. Font size, line height and paragraph anchors repaginate consistently.

### RDR-04 — Prevent OS text selection/copy UX
Disable stock WebView selection handles, default contextual Copy/Share/Translate/Search and selection ActionMode **inside the app** using scoped CSS `user-select:none`, JS gesture guards and native WebView long-press/action-mode handling as appropriate. Preserve ALL first-party word-tap dictionary, per-sentence translation, bookmarks, image zoom, keyboard/TalkBack usability and input fields/settings editing. Never rasterise prose, remove accessible text semantics, or leak content to clipboard. Device-level screenshot OCR, outside apps, or camera OCR cannot be guaranteed preventable by app UI logic: explicitly document this limit rather than falsely promise DRM.

### RDR-05 — Remove reading progress strip
Remove/hide the visible story reading-position progress bar **at the top within stories**. Do not erase actual internal `readpage_*` / `readpage_anchor_*`, chapter progress data or restore ability; any hidden page index required for engine stays functional. Verify across fullscreen, translation, chapter changes and phone/landscape.

### RDR-06 + RDR-10 — Full-canvas reading and quiet metadata
Reader surface gets nearly all available screen; remove persistent labels, redundant instructions, badges, preview copy, crowded chapter ribbons and unnecessary settings/reader controls. Keep important legal credits, book-source provenance and attribution in discreet accessible info/details; do not remove required licence records or original source labels. Settings/search/word tools open contextually rather than cover pages. Preserve UX for reader exit and accessibility.

### RDR-07 — **Exactly SIX app themes** (fixed order)
`liquid-27`, `aurora`, `aqua`, `nebula`, `paper-atlas`, `midnight-library`. Labels: Liquid Glass 27, Liquid Aurora, Aqua Glass, Nebula Flow, Paper Atlas, Midnight Library. Existing shell `themeNames` contains ~58; reduce visible and eligible app themes to six, migrating a removed saved `uiTheme` safely to the nearest retained/default theme. Audit old theme CSS/scripts, user-made colours and outdated tests before deleting unused assets; no unneeded 58-theme screenshots. Six **reader paper presets are distinct from these six app themes.** Do not accidentally remove genre/story-specific source artwork.

### RDR-08 — Premium UI sound effects enabled by default
Current `lingua-licensed-audio.js` has 20 licensed ambience loops + 4 SFX; DEFAULT `sfxEnabled:false`, `ambienceEnabled:false`. New installs: subtle SFX **ON by default** (respect prior explicit user choice during migration; a manual OFF stays OFF), with independent volume, mute-all and Android audio focus. Do not auto-start continuous ambient tracks. Reevaluate all SFX to map sound to actual event: book open, successful paper fold/turn, menu tap, bookmark/save, subtle navigation; no loud clock, bird or intrusive sound for unrelated actions. Prevent overlapping rapid taps, double playback or playback on cancelled gesture. Fold engine emits `turnStart`, `turnCommit`, `turnCancel` or equivalent **single authoritative events**; play a pleasing short paper turn at visual turnover/commit, at most once. No sound on aborted/rebounded page. Bundle assets offline + provenance/licence manifest.

### RDR-09 — Replace stressful ambience
Review and prune current Meta/Oculus CC-BY 4.0 loops and synthetic `lingua-ambience.js` tracks that sound harsh, repetitive, distracting or sleep-disruptive (alarm/chimes/thunder/crowd/ticks). Research alternative soft real recordings: Freesound **item-level CC0** or CC BY with correct credit, Mixkit **actual applicable SFX licence**, appropriate Pixabay sounds after licence review; note neither source is blanket-clearance. Source: https://freesound.org/help/faq/ ; https://mixkit.co/license/modal/sfxFree/ ; https://pixabay.com/blog/posts/free-and-high-quality-sound-effects-for-video-edit-453/ . Use smooth loop points/crossfade, quiet EQ/peak control, no audible seams, no unexpected volume, and no background autoplay. Offline test 5+ minutes per shortlisted loop via real headphones. Store exact original URL, author, licence, checksum, processing in `docs/ASSET_PROVENANCE_AUDIO.md`; remove unused noisy assets from APK only after review.

### RDR-11 — Geometric centring of every button/icon
Audit launcher-side and in-app buttons, pills, back arrows, floating buttons, swipe handles, drawers and settings at 360/600/800/1280 dp widths, LTR and RTL; make icon's optical centre match circle's visual centre with 44dp+ hit targets, consistent inset and no text clipping. Test different font scaling and Android system bars. Never fake centring by aligning only the bounding box.

### RDR-12 — Handwriting-style Arabic + English font collection, app-wide
Current 10 offline OFL fonts in `app/src/main/assets/fonts` and `lt-offline-fonts.css`; expand carefully without duplicate assets. Candidates: Arabic Aref Ruqaa, traditional Naskh, Reem Kufi Ink / other legible OFL handwritten scripts; English Caveat, Patrick Hand and suitable legible book-script alternatives. Verify individual licences and source files; maintain licence texts, bundle **offline**, no runtime Google Fonts. Change AR/EN typography across headings, chapter prose, companions, dictionary/word sheets, bookmarks, menus/settings and search, preserving native icon glyphs and explicit per-language bidi. One shared central font mapping; changing a font repaginates without losing saved paragraph anchor or breaking diacritics. Do not apply handwriting style to icons.

### RDR-13 — Retain approved launcher icon, safer centring
Use existing approved `app/src/main/res/drawable-nodpi/lingua_tales_approved.png` as visual reference and existing adaptive `mipmap-anydpi-v26/ic_launcher.xml`, `drawable/lingua_icon_*`. Keep luminous book/petals mark, NO extra icon text per latest user feedback, slightly reduce symbol scale and harsh contrast, centre inside Android adaptive-icon safe area on circle/squircle/rounded masks; verify screenshot against approved art before altering. No new logo generation or new launcher identity by default.
