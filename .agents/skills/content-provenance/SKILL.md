# content-provenance

Use authorized source editions and provenance; do not invent or abridge originals. Preserve original language and complete contextual companion where requested. Separate candidates from rights-approved and editorially verified content. For Quran never generate or alter verse text; compare with verified source. For medical notes ground claims in assigned references, cite pages, separate external additions and verify clinical assertions. Never upload private records. Avoid reprocessing existing items; record hashes and gaps.
